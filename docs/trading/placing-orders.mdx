This guide walks you through the process of placing orders on ProBioMarkets, from selecting a market to executing your trade.

## Prerequisites

Before placing an order, ensure you have:

- ✅ **Active wallet** with sufficient balance
- ✅ **Selected a market** you want to trade
- ✅ **Decided on your position** (Yes or No, Buy or Sell)

## Order Placement Process

### Step 1: Access the Trading Panel

You can access the trading panel from:

1. **Market Page**: Click any market to view its dedicated page
2. **Markets List**: Select a market to see details in the right sidebar
3. **Portfolio**: Click on an existing position to manage it

### Step 2: Select Outcome

Choose which outcome you want to trade:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Yes" icon="trending-up">
    Buy if you believe the event will happen. Sell if you believe it won't.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No" icon="trending-down">
    Buy if you believe the event won't happen. Sell if you believe it will.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The selected outcome determines which shares you're trading.

### Step 3: Choose Side

Decide whether to **Buy** or **Sell**:

- **Buy**: You're betting the outcome will be correct
- **Sell**: You're betting the outcome will be incorrect (shorting)

<Info>
  Selling is equivalent to buying the opposite outcome. Selling "Yes" is the same as buying "No".
</Info>

### Step 4: Set Amount

Enter the amount you want to invest:

- **Minimum**: Usually $1.00 USDC
- **Maximum**: Your available balance
- **Quick buttons**: Use preset amounts (10, 25, 50, 100)

<Warning>
  Remember that leverage multiplies your position size. A $10 trade with 5x leverage controls $50 worth of shares.
</Warning>

### Step 5: Configure Leverage (Optional)

Set your leverage multiplier:

- **1x**: No leverage (recommended for beginners)
- **2x-5x**: Moderate leverage
- **6x-10x**: High leverage (higher risk)

<Note>
  Higher leverage increases both potential profits and losses. Start conservatively.
</Note>

### Step 6: Select Order Type

Choose between Market and Limit orders:

#### Market Order

- Executes immediately
- Uses best available price
- May have slight slippage
- Best for: Quick execution

#### Limit Order

- Executes only at your price or better
- More price control
- May not fill if price doesn't reach limit
- Best for: Specific entry points

### Step 7: Review Order Summary

Before placing, review:

- **Outcome**: Yes or No
- **Side**: Buy or Sell
- **Amount**: Your investment
- **Leverage**: Position multiplier
- **Current Price**: Market price
- **Potential Payout**: If you win
- **Potential Profit**: Net profit after costs
- **Max Loss**: Maximum possible loss

### Step 8: Execute Order

Click **"Place Order"** to execute:

1. Order is submitted to the order book
2. If market order: Executes immediately
3. If limit order: Waits for matching price
4. Position appears in your portfolio

## Order Confirmation

After placing an order, you'll see:

- ✅ **Confirmation message**
- 📊 **Updated position** in portfolio
- 💰 **Updated balance**
- 📈 **Real-time P&L** tracking

## Managing Open Orders

### Viewing Orders

Check your open orders:

1. Go to **Portfolio** page
2. Click **"Open Orders"** tab
3. View pending limit orders

### Canceling Orders

To cancel a pending limit order:

1. Find the order in your portfolio
2. Click **"Cancel"** button
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Funds are returned to your balance

## Order Execution

### Market Order Execution

Market orders execute in this order:

1. **Best available price** is selected
2. **Order book** is matched
3. **Shares** are allocated
4. **Position** is created
5. **Balance** is updated

### Limit Order Execution

Limit orders wait until:

1. **Price reaches** your limit
2. **Order matches** with opposite side
3. **Trade executes** automatically
4. **Position** is created

<Info>
  Limit orders remain active until they fill, you cancel them, or the market closes.
</Info>

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Quick Trade

**Goal**: Enter position quickly

1. Select market
2. Choose outcome (Yes/No)
3. Select **Market** order
4. Enter amount
5. Click "Place Order"

**Result**: Position opened immediately

### Scenario 2: Specific Entry Price

**Goal**: Enter at a specific price

1. Select market
2. Choose outcome
3. Select **Limit** order
4. Set limit price
5. Enter amount
6. Click "Place Order"

**Result**: Order waits for price to reach limit

### Scenario 3: Leveraged Position

**Goal**: Amplify position size

1. Select market and outcome
2. Enter base amount (e.g., $10)
3. Set leverage (e.g., 5x)
4. Review potential payout
5. Place order

**Result**: $10 controls $50 position

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Order not executing">
    - Check if you have sufficient balance
    - Verify order type (limit orders may not fill immediately)
    - Check if market is still active
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Insufficient balance">
    - Deposit more funds to your wallet
    - Reduce order amount
    - Close existing positions to free up capital
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Price moved before execution">
    - This is normal for market orders (slippage)
    - Use limit orders for price control
    - Check order book depth before trading
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
