Trading on ProBioMarkets is straightforward once you understand the basics. This guide covers the essentials of placing trades, managing positions, and understanding your portfolio.

## Placing Your First Trade

### Step 1: Select a Market

Browse available markets and select one you want to trade:

1. Navigate to the **Markets** page
2. Use filters to find markets by category, volume, or search
3. Click on a market card to view details

### Step 2: Choose Your Outcome

Decide whether you think the event will happen:

- **Yes**: Buy if you believe the event will occur
- **No**: Buy if you believe the event will not occur

### Step 3: Set Your Order

Configure your trade:

- **Amount**: How much you want to invest (in USDC)
- **Leverage**: Optional leverage multiplier (1x to 10x)
- **Order Type**: Market (immediate) or Limit (specific price)
- **Side**: Buy or Sell

### Step 4: Review and Execute

Before placing your order:

- Check the **potential payout** and **profit**
- Review the **current price** and **order book**
- Confirm you have sufficient balance

Click **"Place Order"** to execute!

## Order Types

### Market Orders

Market orders execute immediately at the best available price:

- ✅ **Fast execution** - Fills immediately
- ⚠️ **Price uncertainty** - May have slippage
- 💡 **Best for**: When speed matters more than exact price

### Limit Orders

Limit orders only execute at your specified price or better:

- ✅ **Price control** - You set the exact price
- ⚠️ **May not fill** - If price doesn't reach your limit
- 💡 **Best for**: When you want a specific entry price

## Understanding Positions

### Opening a Position

When you buy shares, you open a position:

```
Buy 10 "Yes" shares at 50¢ each = $5.00
Position: Long Yes (10 shares @ 50¢)
```

### Position P&L

Your profit/loss updates in real-time:

- **Unrealized P&L**: Current profit/loss if you closed now
- **Entry Price**: Price you bought/sold at
- **Current Price**: Current market price
- **Leverage**: Multiplier applied to your position

### Closing a Position

To close a position:

1. Go to **Portfolio** page
2. Find your position
3. Click **"Close Position"**
4. Confirm the trade

<Info>
  You can also close positions from the market page trading panel.
</Info>

## Leverage Trading

Leverage allows you to control larger positions with less capital:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Leverage Works">
    With 5x leverage, $10 can control a $50 position. If the price moves 10% in your favor, you make 50% profit (minus fees).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Leverage Risks">
    Leverage amplifies losses too. A 10% move against you with 5x leverage results in a 50% loss. Use stop-losses and proper risk management.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Available Leverage">
    ProBioMarkets supports 1x (no leverage) up to 10x leverage. Start with lower leverage until you're comfortable.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Risk Management

<Warning>
  Always trade with money you can afford to lose. Prediction markets are speculative and carry risk.
</Warning>

### Best Practices

1. **Start Small**: Begin with small positions to learn
2. **Diversify**: Don't put all capital in one market
3. **Use Stop-Losses**: Set limits on maximum losses
4. **Research**: Understand the markets you're trading
5. **Manage Leverage**: Higher leverage = higher risk
