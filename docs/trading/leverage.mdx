Leverage allows you to control a larger position size with less capital, amplifying both potential profits and losses.

## What is Leverage?

Leverage multiplies your position size:

- **1x leverage**: No leverage (1:1)
- **2x leverage**: $10 controls $20 position
- **5x leverage**: $10 controls $50 position
- **10x leverage**: $10 controls $100 position

<Warning>
  Leverage amplifies losses. A 10% move against you with 5x leverage results in a 50% loss.
</Warning>

## How Leverage Works

### Without Leverage

```
Capital: $10
Leverage: 1x
Position Size: $10
Price Movement: +10%
Profit: $1 (10% return)
```

### With 5x Leverage

```
Capital: $10
Leverage: 5x
Position Size: $50
Price Movement: +10%
Profit: $5 (50% return)
```

**Same capital, 5x the profit!**

## Leverage Example

### Scenario: Bitcoin Price Prediction

**Market**: "Will Bitcoin reach $100k by 2025?"
**Current Yes Price**: 50¢
**Your Capital**: $100

#### Without Leverage (1x)

- Buy 200 shares at 50¢ = $100
- If price goes to 60¢: Profit = $20 (20% return)
- If price goes to 40¢: Loss = $20 (20% loss)

#### With 5x Leverage

- Buy 1,000 shares at 50¢ = $100 capital controls $500 position
- If price goes to 60¢: Profit = $100 (100% return)
- If price goes to 40¢: Loss = $100 (100% loss)

## Leverage Risks

### Amplified Losses

Leverage works both ways:

```
5x Leverage, 20% price move against you:
Loss = 100% of your capital
```

### Liquidation Risk

If your position moves too far against you:

- **Margin call**: May need to add funds
- **Liquidation**: Position may be closed automatically
- **Total loss**: Possible to lose entire position

### Volatility Impact

High leverage + volatile markets = higher risk:

- **Small price moves** = large P&L swings
- **Emotional stress** from rapid changes
- **Harder to manage** positions

## Risk Management

### Start Small

- Begin with **1x-2x leverage**
- Learn the platform first
- Gradually increase as you gain experience

### Use Stop-Losses

Set automatic exit points:

```
Entry: 50¢ with 5x leverage
Stop-loss: 45¢
Max loss: Limited to 25% of capital
```

### Position Sizing

Don't over-leverage:

- **Conservative**: 1x-2x leverage
- **Moderate**: 3x-5x leverage
- **Aggressive**: 6x-10x leverage (high risk)

### Diversification

Don't put all capital in one leveraged position:

- **Spread risk** across multiple markets
- **Limit leverage** per position
- **Maintain reserves** for opportunities

## Leverage Strategies

### Conservative Approach

- **Leverage**: 1x-2x
- **Focus**: Capital preservation
- **Best for**: Beginners, risk-averse traders

### Moderate Approach

- **Leverage**: 3x-5x
- **Focus**: Balanced risk/reward
- **Best for**: Experienced traders

### Aggressive Approach

- **Leverage**: 6x-10x
- **Focus**: Maximum returns
- **Best for**: Very experienced, high-risk tolerance

## Calculating Potential Returns

### Profit Calculation

```
Profit = (Price Change %) × Leverage × Capital

Example:
Capital: $100
Leverage: 5x
Price Change: +10%
Profit = 10% × 5 × $100 = $50
```

### Loss Calculation

```
Loss = (Price Change %) × Leverage × Capital

Example:
Capital: $100
Leverage: 5x
Price Change: -10%
Loss = 10% × 5 × $100 = $50
```

## When to Use Leverage

### Good Use Cases

- ✅ **High conviction** trades
- ✅ **Liquid markets** with tight spreads
- ✅ **Short-term** positions
- ✅ **Small position sizes** relative to capital

### Avoid Leverage When

- ❌ **Uncertain** about outcome
- ❌ **Illiquid markets** (wide spreads)
- ❌ **Long-term holds** (time decay)
- ❌ **Large positions** (high risk)
