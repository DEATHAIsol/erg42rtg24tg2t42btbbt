Parlays allow you to combine multiple prediction markets into a single bet, multiplying your potential returns if all outcomes are correct.

## What are Parlays?

A parlay combines 2 or more markets where **all outcomes must be correct** to win:

```
Parlay Example:
- Market A: Yes (60¢)
- Market B: Yes (70¢)
- Market C: No (55¢)

Combined Odds: 0.60 × 0.70 × 0.55 = 0.231 (23.1¢)
```

If all three outcomes are correct, you win. If any one is wrong, you lose.

## How Parlays Work

### Combining Markets

Each market in a parlay has its own probability:

```
Market 1: 60% chance (60¢)
Market 2: 70% chance (70¢)
Market 3: 55% chance (55¢)

Combined Probability: 60% × 70% × 55% = 23.1%
```

The combined probability is **lower** than individual markets, but payouts are **higher**.

### Payout Calculation

```
Stake: $100
Combined Odds: 0.231 (23.1¢)
Payout: $100 / 0.231 = $432.90
Profit: $332.90
```

<Info>
  All legs must win for the parlay to pay out. One wrong outcome = entire parlay loses.
</Info>

## Building a Parlay

### Step 1: Add Markets

1. Go to **Parlays** page
2. Click **"Add Market"**
3. Browse and select markets
4. Choose outcome (Yes/No) for each

### Step 2: Review Legs

Each leg shows:

- **Market question**
- **Selected outcome** (Yes/No)
- **Current price**
- **Remove option**

### Step 3: Set Stake

Enter your stake amount:

- **Minimum**: Usually $1.00
- **Maximum**: Your available balance
- **Quick buttons**: Preset amounts

### Step 4: Calculate Payout

The calculator shows:

- **Combined odds**: Total probability
- **Potential payout**: If all legs win
- **Potential profit**: Net profit after stake

### Step 5: Place Parlay

Review and place:

- ✅ All legs correct
- ✅ Stake amount
- ✅ Potential returns
- ✅ Risk (lose all if one wrong)

## Parlay Example

### Three-Leg Parlay

```
Leg 1: "Bitcoin reaches $100k" - Yes (50¢)
Leg 2: "Ethereum reaches $5k" - Yes (60¢)
Leg 3: "Stock market up 10%" - No (40¢)

Combined Odds: 0.50 × 0.60 × 0.40 = 0.12 (12¢)
Stake: $100
Potential Payout: $833.33
Potential Profit: $733.33
```

**If all three happen**: Win $833.33
**If any one fails**: Lose $100

## Parlay Strategies

### Conservative Parlays

- **Fewer legs**: 2-3 markets
- **Higher probabilities**: Markets with 60%+ odds
- **Lower risk**: Better chance all win
- **Moderate returns**: Still multiplied but safer

### Aggressive Parlays

- **More legs**: 4-6+ markets
- **Lower probabilities**: Markets with 40-50% odds
- **Higher risk**: Harder for all to win
- **High returns**: Massive multipliers if successful

### Balanced Approach

- **Mix of probabilities**: Some high, some medium
- **3-4 legs**: Good balance of risk/reward
- **Diversified markets**: Different categories
- **Reasonable stake**: Don't bet the farm

## Risk Considerations

<Warning>
  Parlays are high-risk. All legs must win, so the probability of success decreases with each additional leg.
</Warning>

### Probability Math

```
2-leg parlay (both 50%):
Success chance: 50% × 50% = 25%

3-leg parlay (all 50%):
Success chance: 50% × 50% × 50% = 12.5%

4-leg parlay (all 50%):
Success chance: 6.25%
```

### Risk Management

- **Start small**: Test with small stakes
- **Limit legs**: More legs = lower success rate
- **Choose wisely**: Select markets you're confident about
- **Don't over-bet**: Parlays should be part of strategy, not all of it

## When to Use Parlays

### Good Use Cases

- ✅ **High conviction** on multiple outcomes
- ✅ **Correlated events** (related markets)
- ✅ **Small stakes** for high potential returns
- ✅ **Diversified portfolio** (part of strategy)

### Avoid Parlays When

- ❌ **Uncertain** about outcomes
- ❌ **Large stakes** (too risky)
- ❌ **Too many legs** (low success probability)
- ❌ **Independent events** with no edge
