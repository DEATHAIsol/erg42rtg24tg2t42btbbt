Welcome to **ProBioMarkets**, a cutting-edge prediction market platform built on Solana. ProBioMarkets enables users to trade on the outcomes of real-world events, from politics and economics to sports and technology.

## What is ProBioMarkets?

ProBioMarkets is a decentralized prediction market platform that allows users to:

- **Trade on outcomes** of real-world events
- **Create markets** on topics you're knowledgeable about
- **Earn yields** by providing liquidity to markets
- **Build parlays** to combine multiple predictions for amplified returns
- **Leverage positions** to maximize potential gains
