ProBioMarkets offers a liquidity pool system where you can deposit SOL and earn yield from market-making activities and staking rewards.

## What is Liquidity?

Liquidity refers to the ability to buy or sell assets quickly without significantly affecting the price. In prediction markets, liquidity providers:

- **Deposit funds** into liquidity pools
- **Enable trading** by providing capital
- **Earn yield** from trading fees and rewards
- **Withdraw** funds when needed

## How It Works

### Liquidity Pools

When you deposit SOL into the liquidity pool:

1. **Funds are pooled** with other providers
2. **Used for market-making** across all markets
3. **Generate returns** from multiple sources
4. **Yield is distributed** to all providers

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Navigate to Liquidity Page

1. Click **"Liquidity"** in the main navigation
2. View the liquidity dashboard
3. See current pool statistics

### Step 2: Review Pool Information

Before depositing, check:

- **Total Pool Value**: Current pool size
- **Your Balance**: Your current deposits
- **APY Breakdown**: Expected yield sources
- **Historical Performance**: Past returns

### Step 3: Deposit SOL

To deposit:

1. Enter **amount** you want to deposit
2. Review **expected yield** calculation
3. Click **"Deposit"**
4. Confirm transaction
5. Funds are added to pool

### Step 4: Monitor Performance

Track your liquidity:

- **Current Balance**: Your deposited amount
- **Accrued Yield**: Earnings so far
- **APY**: Current annual percentage yield
- **Withdrawal Options**: Available anytime

## Withdrawing Funds

### Withdrawal Process

1. Go to **Liquidity** page
2. Enter **withdrawal amount**
3. Click **"Withdraw"**
4. Confirm transaction
5. Funds return to your wallet

<Info>
  Withdrawals are typically processed immediately. Your yield is included in the withdrawal amount.
</Info>

### Withdrawal Considerations

- **No lock-up period**: Withdraw anytime
- **Yield included**: Your earnings are part of withdrawal
- **Gas fees**: Small transaction fees apply
- **Processing time**: Usually instant

## Risk Considerations

<Warning>
  Providing liquidity involves risks. Always understand the risks before depositing funds.
</Warning>

### Risks to Consider

- **Smart contract risk**: Protocol vulnerabilities
- **Market risk**: Changes in market conditions
- **Liquidity risk**: Ability to withdraw quickly
- **Yield variability**: APY can change over time

### Risk Mitigation

- **Start small**: Test with smaller amounts
- **Diversify**: Don't put all funds in one pool
- **Monitor regularly**: Check performance
- **Understand terms**: Read all documentation
