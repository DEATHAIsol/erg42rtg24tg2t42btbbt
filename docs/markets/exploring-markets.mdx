## Filtering Markets

### Category Filters

Filter markets by category:

1. Open the **Sidebar** (filter panel)
2. Select one or more **categories**
3. Markets update automatically
4. Clear filters to reset

### Volume & Liquidity Filters

Set minimum thresholds:

- **Minimum Volume**: Filter by trading volume
- **Minimum Liquidity**: Filter by available liquidity

<Info>
  Higher volume and liquidity typically mean more accurate prices and easier trading.
</Info>

### Search Functionality

Search markets by:

- **Question text**: Market title
- **Description**: Market details
- **Tags**: Category tags
- **Keywords**: Any relevant terms

## Market Information

### Market Card Details

Each market card shows:

- **Question**: The event being predicted
- **Category Tags**: Market classification
- **Current Price**: Yes/No prices
- **Volume**: Total trading volume
- **Price Change**: 24h price movement
- **Image**: Visual identifier (if available)

### Market Page

Click a market to see:

- **Full Description**: Detailed market information
- **Price Chart**: Historical price data
- **Order Book**: Live bids and asks
- **Trading Panel**: Place orders
- **Market Stats**: Volume, liquidity, end date

## Sorting Options

Sort markets by:

- **Volume** (default): Highest trading volume first
- **Liquidity**: Most liquid markets first
- **Newest**: Recently created markets
- **Oldest**: Longest-running markets

## Market Quality Indicators

### High-Quality Markets

Look for markets with:

- ✅ **Clear resolution criteria**
- ✅ **High volume** (active trading)
- ✅ **Good liquidity** (tight spreads)
- ✅ **Reasonable end date** (not too far out)
- ✅ **Objective outcomes** (verifiable)

### Markets to Avoid

Be cautious of markets with:

- ⚠️ **Vague questions** (unclear outcomes)
- ⚠️ **Low liquidity** (wide spreads, hard to trade)
- ⚠️ **Subjective resolution** (disputable outcomes)
- ⚠️ **Very long timeframes** (years away)

## Market Selection Tips

### For Trading

Choose markets with:

1. **Active trading** - High volume and liquidity
2. **Clear outcomes** - Easy to verify resolution
3. **Reasonable timeframe** - Not too far in future
4. **Your expertise** - Markets you understand

### For Information

Use markets to:

- **Gauge sentiment** on events
- **Track probabilities** over time
- **Discover trends** in public opinion
- **Research topics** you're interested in

## Market Navigation

### Quick Actions

From any market:

- **Click market card**: View full details
- **Select in sidebar**: See details panel
- **Expand button**: Open full market page
- **Share**: Copy market link

### Market Pages

Full market pages include:

- **Header**: Market question and image
- **Description**: Full market details
- **Price Display**: Yes/No prices with buy/sell
- **Chart**: Historical price data
- **Order Book**: Live order book
- **Trading Panel**: Place orders
- **Your Positions**: Your open positions
