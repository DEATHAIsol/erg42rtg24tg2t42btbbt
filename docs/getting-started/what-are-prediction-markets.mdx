Prediction markets are exchange-traded markets created for the purpose of trading the outcome of events. They allow participants to buy and sell shares based on their predictions of future events.

## Core Concept

In a prediction market, you can:

1. **Buy shares** if you believe an event will happen
2. **Sell shares** if you believe an event won't happen
3. **Trade shares** at any time before the event resolves
4. **Profit** if your prediction is correct

## How It Works

### Binary Markets

Most prediction markets are **binary**, meaning they have two possible outcomes:

- **Yes** - The event will happen
- **No** - The event will not happen

Each outcome has a price between $0.00 and $1.00 (or 0¢ and 100¢). These prices represent the market's collective probability that the event will occur.

<Info>
  If "Yes" shares are trading at 75¢, the market believes there's a 75% chance the event will happen.
</Info>

### Example

Let's say there's a market: **"Will it rain tomorrow?"**

- **Yes shares** are trading at **60¢** (60% probability)
- **No shares** are trading at **40¢** (40% probability)

If you buy 10 Yes shares at 60¢ each:
- **Cost**: $6.00
- If it rains: Each share pays out $1.00 = **$10.00 profit** ($4.00 net)
- If it doesn't rain: Each share pays out $0.00 = **$6.00 loss**

## Market Resolution

Markets resolve when the event outcome is determined:

1. **Automatic Resolution**: Based on verifiable data sources
2. **Manual Resolution**: By market creators or designated oracles
3. **Dispute Resolution**: Through community governance if needed

When a market resolves:
- **Winning shares** pay out $1.00 each
- **Losing shares** pay out $0.00
- Traders can claim their winnings immediately
