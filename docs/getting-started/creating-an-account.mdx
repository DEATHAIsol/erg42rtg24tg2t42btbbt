Getting started with ProBioMarkets is quick and easy. This guide will walk you through account creation and wallet setup.

## Account Setup

ProBioMarkets uses a **custodial wallet system** that automatically creates a Solana wallet for you when you first visit the platform.

### First-Time Setup

1. **Visit** [probiomarkets.com](https://probiomarkets.com)
2. **Navigate** to any page that requires wallet access
3. **Automatic wallet creation** - A new Solana wallet is generated for you
4. **Backup your wallet** - You'll be prompted to save your private key

<Warning>
  **CRITICAL**: Always backup your private key! If you lose it, you cannot recover your wallet or funds.
</Warning>

### Wallet Onboarding

When your wallet is first created, you'll see an onboarding flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Your new wallet address is displayed. This is your unique identifier on the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Backup Private Key">
    You'll be shown your private key. **Save this securely** - it's the only way to access your wallet from other devices or if you clear browser data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Options">
    You can export your wallet as:
    - **Private key text file**
    - **JSON wallet file**
    - **Copy to clipboard**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ready to Trade">
    Once you've backed up your wallet, you're ready to start trading!
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wallet Management

### Viewing Your Wallet

Your wallet information is always accessible via the wallet button in the top navigation:

- **Balance**: Current SOL balance
- **Address**: Your wallet's public address
- **Actions**: Deposit, export, view on Solscan

### Depositing Funds

To deposit SOL into your wallet:

1. Click the **"Deposit"** button in the wallet menu
2. **Copy your wallet address** or scan the QR code
3. **Send SOL** from an external wallet or exchange
4. **Wait for confirmation** (usually 1-2 minutes)

<Info>
  You can also view your wallet address on Solscan by clicking the external link icon.
</Info>

### Exporting Your Wallet

To export your wallet for backup or use on another device:

1. Click the **key icon** in the wallet menu
2. Choose export format:
   - **Private Key**: Plain text file
   - **Wallet JSON**: Complete wallet file
3. **Save securely** - Never share your private key!

### Security Best Practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Backup Regularly" icon="shield">
    Keep multiple backups of your private key in secure locations
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never Share Keys" icon="lock">
    Never share your private key with anyone - not even support
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use Secure Storage" icon="vault">
    Store backups offline in encrypted storage or hardware wallets
  </Card>
  <Card title="Verify Addresses" icon="check">
    Always double-check wallet addresses before sending funds
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Paper Trading

ProBioMarkets includes a **paper trading** feature for practice:

- Start with **0.5 SOL** of virtual funds
- Practice trading without real money
- Learn the platform risk-free
- Track your performance

<Note>
  Paper trading uses simulated funds and positions. Real trading requires actual SOL deposits.
</Note>
